Understanding Your Personal Injury Claim

When you are injured due to someone else's negligence, the legal and financial aftermath can be as painful as the injury itself. Understanding your rights is the first step toward recovery.

A personal injury claim allows a person who has been injured to seek compensation (or "damages") from the person or entity responsible for the harm. This can include medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering.

What Constitutes a Claim?

Most claims are based on negligence. We must prove that the other party had a duty of care, breached that duty, and that breach directly caused your injuries and subsequent damages.

"Insurance companies are not on your side. Their goal is to pay as little as possible. Our goal is to make them pay what is fair and just. We are your advocate."

Our Process: Investigation to Resolution

We immediately begin collecting evidence, speaking to witnesses, and consulting with experts. We handle all communication with the insurance companies, build a powerful case, and are fully prepared to go to trial if a fair settlement is not offered.

Your Rights Are Paramount

From the moment of your arrest, your constitutional rights are in effect. Understanding them is your first line of defense.

  • The right to remain silent. Use it.
  • The right to an attorney. Demand one.
  • The right to be free from unreasonable searches.
