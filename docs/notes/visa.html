4. Simple Takeaway

If you want to… Best Fit
Build high-scale systems with real algorithms and financial logic Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, PayPal
Do tech consulting, client work, audits, risk assessment KPMG, Deloitte, PwC, EY, BDO, RSM
Work deeply in finance + software engineering Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs Engineering
Work across many industries with a tech advisory role Big Four + mid-tier consulting firms

In short: Visa-like companies are ideal if you want deep engineering in finance; KPMG/BDO/Big Four are ideal if you want tech + business consulting across many clients and industries.
