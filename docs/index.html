AlphaVi Trading Bot

Mar 2025 — Present
Project Overview

AlphaVi is a solo-built quantitative trading framework for researching, deciding, and automating equity and ETF positions from one local runtime.
The design treats trading as a pipeline. Broker and market feeds are consolidated into a single picture of cash, open positions, and live prices. Analytical layers interpret that state against historical behavior, and execution applies sizing and timing rules across the session.
Rather than a single script, the codebase is packaged as reusable Python libraries so data ingestion, predictive modeling, and order workflows can evolve independently while sharing the same internal data contract.
The project emphasizes modularity, repeatable scheduling, and regime-aware decision making (market conditions, long vs short posture) before any order is sent to the broker.

Responsibilities

  • Integrated Alpaca broker API, FTP, and market data feeds into one ingestion layer that normalizes account, position, and pricing signals.
  • Built regression models on historical price and position data to rank long and short candidates and guide trade timing and sizing.
  • Built a scheduler-driven pipeline with timed jobs and accounting-based buy/sell rules from normalized account and market data.
